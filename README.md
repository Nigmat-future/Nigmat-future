# Nigmat

Biomedical AI builder focused on agentic research systems, translational bioinformatics, and scientific developer tooling.

I work on systems that help turn ambiguous biomedical questions into reproducible analyses, publishable study designs, and usable research products.

## Selected work

### [bioagent](https://github.com/Nigmat-future/bioagent)
Autonomous multi-agent system for end-to-end bioinformatics research.

### [biomedical-skill-suite](https://github.com/Nigmat-future/biomedical-skill-suite)
A layered biomedical skill library for study design, public-cohort planning, omics workflows, validation, and manuscript-oriented research packaging.

### [biomedical-codex-skills](https://github.com/Nigmat-future/biomedical-codex-skills)
A publishable multi-skill repository for biomedical AI coding and agent workflows.

### [publishable-research-orchestrator](https://github.com/Nigmat-future/publishable-research-orchestrator)
Codex skill for multi-agent research discovery, publishability scoring, and execution planning.

### [pretext-med](https://github.com/Nigmat-future/pretext-med)
AI-powered clinical report generator built around streaming-first clinical UI constraints.

### [cellhop](https://github.com/Nigmat-future/cellhop)
Interoperability tooling for moving between AnnData and Seurat without awkward manual conversion steps.

## Areas I keep returning to

- agentic biomedical research systems
- public-dataset study design and validation strategy
- single-cell, spatial, imaging, and multi-omics workflows
- clinical AI interfaces under real-world product constraints
- scientific tooling that removes friction from research execution

## Now

Right now I am building out a more serious biomedical research tooling stack around:
- modular Codex skills for biomedical study design
- publishable workflows for TCGA, GEO, omics, imaging, and spatial projects
- agent systems that help move from idea to research plan to executable artifact
